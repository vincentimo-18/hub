Colombian animation and creative director with seventeen years in the industry.

Lead and senior animator with 17+ years in VFX and animation, and an AI filmmaker since 2025.

Recognized for contributions to Disney's Mufasa, Netflix's Avatar: The Last Airbender and Apple TV+'s Prehistoric Planet. Animation work featured in award-winning productions including Avengers: Infinity War (Academy Award-nominated, VES Award-winning), Christopher Robin (Academy Award-nominated) and His Dark Materials (BAFTA-winning).

Now pairing that craft with generative AI production as founder and director of London creative studio Beat Maps Squad: director of award-winning AI short films (Between the Lines, The Wind Changes, The Immigrants), creator of the original IP LLAQTA, and Creative Partner for Magnific, Dreamina, Topaz Labs and CapCut.
